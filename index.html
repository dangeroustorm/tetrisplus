Operation Guide

Experience the evolution of geometry and physics. As you score, the universe distorts.

  • Lvl 1 (Standard): Normal Euclidean Space.
  • Lvl 2 (Transforms): Canvas perspective shifts continuously.
  • Lvl 3 (Topology): Space becomes a Torus. Pieces wrap around edges.
  • Lvl 4 (Probability): Quantum pieces fluctuate until observed (moved).
  • Lvl 5 (Chaos): Grid space warps. Visual chaos.

Controls: Keyboard Arrows (Mobile: Touch buttons). Up to Rotate, Down to Drop.
